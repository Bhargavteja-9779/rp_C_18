# Style notes (own words, from titles/abstracts only)
- Titles: ~15 words, often a method label plus a water target; colon-free.
- Abstracts: ~230 words; start with the water problem, then the gap, then what was built, then a quantified result, then a use.
- Plain, active sentences; define terms once; hedge claims; end with limits.
- Not studied (UNVERIFIED): section names, reference style, figure style, contribution lists.
