# R3 - the skeptical statistician
Attempts to make the gains disappear (code/src/statistician_attack.py, Table S-A): Wilcoxon, paired t, sign test, sign-flip permutation, drop-one-state jackknife, drop the 5 largest or 5 smallest states, each seed alone, well-weighted mean difference.
Findings: arsenic>10 feedback vs map survives everything (largest p 0.0001). Arsenic>5 feedback vs map is borderline (sign test p 0.07; without the 5 largest states p 0.10; worst single seed 0.08), but ABF vs map is clear (p<=0.0004). Fluoride>2 feedback vs map: borderline (worst single seed 0.098); ABF clear. Fluoride>4 and uranium: mostly not significant (17, 9 and 12 states). Weighting by number of wells shrinks differences but keeps the sign.
Other points: (a) states are not independent (neighbouring aquifers): p-values may be optimistic; leave-one-region-out was kept significant for arsenic>10; 4 regions are too few for a cluster bootstrap. (b) tuning not nested; 8 tuning states. (c) ABF 'never significantly worse' is an absence of evidence with 9-31 units; the Letter reports the worst gap with its p (0.057). (d) 8 + ideas tried in elevation: counted and reported (2 tested). (e) mixed-effects model across states and seeds NOT fitted (UNVERIFIED benefit).
Fixes: stress-test results added to the Letter and SI; claims for plain feedback outside arsenic>10 are described as borderline.
