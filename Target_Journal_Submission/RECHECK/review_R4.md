# R4 - the practitioner (county environmental-health programme officer)
Would I use it? The rule 'test near wells that just came back unsafe' is intuitive and I might already do it; the numbers (9.2 tests per unsafe well fell to 5.2 in simulation) are in a unit I can budget with. I would use the follow-unsafe rule, and ABF only with my own history.
What I need that is missing: (1) laboratory turnaround: results back in small batches; the paper shows the gain shrinks with 200 wells per round and ABF is more sensitive; real labs return results in weeks. (2) owner refusals and access: tested by hiding 30% of wells (no loss), but not tested as non-random refusal. (3) cost per test and travel are not modelled. (4) the wells in the study are USGS study wells, not private wells chosen by owners. (5) no field pilot; I would not change my protocol on a simulation alone. (6) a usable tool: code/campaign_planner.py exists and is tested, but it is a command-line script without validation.
