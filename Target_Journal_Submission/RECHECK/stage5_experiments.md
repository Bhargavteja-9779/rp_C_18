1. Clean rerun: main arsenic>10 experiment re-run in a fresh virtual environment (installed from requirements.txt; numpy 2.5.5, pandas 3.1.1 vs 2.5.3, 3.0.5): tuned parameters, tuning states and every per-state, per-method result identical (largest difference 0.0000). results/recheck/clean_rerun_as10.json. 2. Leakage hunt: buffer sweep (0, 5, 25, 100 km) gives within-state AUC 0.525, 0.521, 0.513, 0.508 (no leakage signal); shuffled-label training gives AUC 0.486 and enrichment 0.92 (chance). Earlier: label shuffling inside the campaign gave 1.01. Predictors known before testing only; earliest sample per well; duplicates by coordinates collapsed; no result-derived feature. Temporal leakage: forward-in-time check (6 states) agrees.
3. Sanity and trivial baselines: random order (1.0 by construction), spreading tests evenly (not better than random), lithology prevalence rule, logistic regression, nearest-neighbour map, four learners (learner_check). No score is suspiciously high (largest enrichment 2.7 for fluoride hybrid).
4. Baseline fairness: same tuning states/budget for map and feedback parameters; online-refit weight checked after the fact; added learners ExtraTrees, RandomForest, Logistic; added published-idea comparators (follow-unsafe = adaptive-cluster-sampling-like; kernel feedback = active-search-like). Not implemented: a full Gaussian-process active-search baseline (Garnett 2012) or BlueConduit-style systems (not public): UNVERIFIED comparison to 'the 3 best published methods'.
5. Seeds: 5 (3 for fluoride and some secondary runs). The static map is never the best method in any seed; method ranking is stable for arsenic>10, >5, fluoride; unstable for uranium (low power).
6. Statistics: paired Wilcoxon with Holm; added sign, t, permutation, jackknife, drop-5-largest/smallest states, single seeds (Table S-A). Feedback gain robust for arsenic>10 only; borderline elsewhere; ABF more robust; uranium unresolved.
7. Robustness: added earlier (thinning, blocks, delay, availability, region, forward-in-time, synthetic). Unfavourable conditions included (late batches hurt ABF most).
8. Efficiency: timings in the SI (feedback 0.3 s vs online refit 8 s on one core); FLOPs/energy not measured.
9. Error analysis: states where maps or feedback fail are listed (e.g. Minnesota, Florida, Washington); explanation is a labelled hypothesis.
10. Calibration: ABF forecast intervals were badly calibrated (coverage 6-27% uncalibrated; recalibration did not give usable intervals) - reported as a negative result.
